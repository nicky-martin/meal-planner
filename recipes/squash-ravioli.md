# Squash Ravioli

tags: Pasta, Butternut, Italian, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Butternut squash ravioli (frozen or fresh)
- Sage butter
- Parmesan cheese
- Nuts (walnuts or pine nuts) - optional
- Salt and pepper

## Instructions
1. Cook ravioli according to package in salted boiling water.
2. Drain and set aside.
3. In a pan, melt butter with fresh sage.
4. Toss ravioli with sage butter.
5. Top with parmesan and nuts if desired.
6. Serve warm.
