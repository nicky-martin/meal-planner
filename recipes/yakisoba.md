# Yakisoba

tags: Japanese, Noodles, Stir-fry, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Yakisoba noodles x2
- Broccol
- Garlic, minced
- Ginger, minced
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil

## Instructions
1. Cook noodles according to package and drain.
2. Heat oil in a wok and stir-fry vegetables.
3. Add garlic, ginger, and cooked noodles.
4. Toss with soy sauce and mirin.
5. Drizzle with sesame oil and top with bonito flakes if using.
