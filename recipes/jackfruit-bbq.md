# Jackfruit BBQ

tags: BBQ, Vegetarian, Jackfruit, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Canned jackfruit
- BBQ sauce
- Slaw
- Buns

## Instructions
1. Drain and rinse canned jackfruit.
2. Shred the jackfruit with two forks.
3. Heat BBQ sauce in a pan and add shredded jackfruit.
4. Simmer 5-10 minutes until heated through.
5. Serve on bread or tortillas with coleslaw.
