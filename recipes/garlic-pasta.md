# Garlic Pasta

tags: Italian, Pasta, Vegetarian, Quick, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Angel hair pasta
- Garlic, 2 cloves
- Olive oil
- Red pepper flakes
- Fresh parsley

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta to al dente, reserving pasta water.
2. In a pan, warm olive oil and add garlic spread or minced garlic.
3. Cook 1-2 minutes until fragrant, add red pepper flakes.
4. Drain pasta and toss with garlic oil, adding pasta water as needed.
5. Top with parmesan and fresh parsley. Serve.
