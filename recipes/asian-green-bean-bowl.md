# Asian Green Bean Bowl

tags: Bowls, Vegetables, Asian, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Green beans (fresh or frozen)
- Garlic, minced
- Ginger, minced
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Sesame seeds
- Rice for serving
- Cashews or peanuts

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a skillet and sauté garlic and ginger.
2. Add green beans and stir-fry 4-5 minutes.
3. Add soy sauce and cook 2 more minutes.
4. Drizzle with sesame oil and sprinkle sesame seeds.
5. Serve over rice with nuts if desired.
