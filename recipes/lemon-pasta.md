# Lemon Pasta

tags: Pasta, Italian, Vegetarian, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Lemon pasta
- Garlic, minced
- Olive oil
- Fresh parsley

## Instructions
1. Cook pasta to al dente, reserving pasta water.
2. In a pan, warm olive oil and sauté garlic.
3. Add lemon juice and zest.
4. Toss drained pasta with lemon mixture, adding pasta water as needed.
5. Top with parmesan and fresh parsley.
6. Serve warm.
