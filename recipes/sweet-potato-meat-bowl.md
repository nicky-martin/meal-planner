# Sweet Potato Meat Bowl

tags: Roasted, Bowls, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Sweet potato, cubed
- Impossible Beef
- Green beans
- Sesame oil
- Lime juice

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Toss sweet potato with olive oil and spices, cumin and chili pepper
3. Roast 20-25 minutes until tender.
4. Warm taco meat.
5. Assemble bowl with roasted sweet potato, meat, and vegetables.
6. Drizzle with lime juice and serve.
