# Quesadilla

tags: Mexican, Cheese, Quick, Lunch, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Flour tortillas
- Mexican cheese blend
- Onion
- Black beans, 
- salsa
- Butter

## Instructions
1. Heat butter in a skillet over medium heat.
2. Place one tortilla in the pan.
3. Add cheese and fillings to one half.
4. Fold tortilla in half.
5. Cook 2-3 minutes until golden, flip and cook other side.
6. Slice and serve with salsa and yogurt.
