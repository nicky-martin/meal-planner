# Asian Stirfry

tags: Asian, Stir-fry, Quick, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Mixed vegetables (broccoli, carrots, snap peas, onion, bell pepper)
- Garlic, minced
- Ginger, minced
- Soy sauce
- Sesame oil
- Rice
- Tofu

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a wok or large skillet over high heat.
2. Add garlic and ginger, stir-fry 30 seconds.
3. Add vegetables and stir-fry 
4. Add soy sauce and optional protein.
5. Cook 2-3 minutes more until vegetables are tender-crisp.
6. Drizzle with sesame oil and serve over rice.
