# Indian Potato Tacos

tags: Indian, Tacos, Vegetarian, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Naan
- Potatos
- Frozen peas
- Red onion, thinly sliced
- Cilantro
- Lime
- Jalapeño

## Instructions
1. Cook diced potatoes with curry powder, turmeric, and cumin.
2. Mash um up a bit
3. Add frozen peas
4. Warm flatbread.
5. Assemble tacos with slurry, curried potatoes, onion, cilantro.
6. Top with yogurt and serve with lime wedges.
