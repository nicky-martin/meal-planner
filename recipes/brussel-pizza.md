# Brussel Pizza

tags: Pizza, Vegetarian, Roasted, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Pizza flatbread
- Shredded brussels sprouts
- Mozzarella cheese
- Garlic spread
- Olive oil
- Balsamic glaze

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 400°F.
2. Spread garlic spread on pizza bread.
3. Toss shredded brussels sprouts with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
4. Spread brussels sprouts on the bread.
5. Top with mozzarella cheese.
6. Bake 15 minutes until cheese is melted and brussels are crispy.
7. Drizzle with balsamic glaze
