# Ramen

tags: Asian, Noodles, Quick, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Ramen noodles
- Broccoli
- Soy sauce
- Ginger
- Garlic
- Green onions
- Frozen peas
- Edamame
- Fake chicken

## Instructions
1. Bring broth to a boil.
2. Add soy sauce, ginger, and garlic.
3. Add ramen noodles and cook according to package.
4. Add peas or edamame and cook 2 more minutes.
5. Crack in egg if desired and stir gently.
6. Top with green onions and serve.
