# Ravioli Broccoli

tags: Pasta, Italian, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Fridge ravioli
- Olive oil
- Garlic, minced
- Fresh basil (optional)

## Instructions
1. Bring salted water to boil and cook ravioli according to package.
2. Drain and return to pot.
3. Toss with olive oil, minced garlic, and parmesan.
4. Add fresh basil if desired.
5. Season to taste and serve warm.
