# Blueberry PB Smoothie

tags: Quick, Smoothie, Breakfast

## Ingredients
- Frozen blueberries
- Banana
- Peanut butter
- Oat milk
- Vanilla protein powder

## Instructions
1. Add milk to blender.
2. Add frozen blueberries, peanut butter, protein powder, and sweetener.
3. Blend until smooth.
4. Pour and serve immediately.
