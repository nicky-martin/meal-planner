# Homemade Harvest Bowl

tags: Bowls, Vegetarian, Roasted, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Steamed or roasted rice
- Roasted sweet potato
- Roasted brussels
- Shredded carots
- Chickpeas
- Kale
- Vinaigrette 
- Sunflower seeds

## Instructions
1. cook the chickpeas
2. roast the sweet potato, then chop it
3. roast the brussles
4. prep the kale, massage in oil
5. assemble and dress
6. top with seeds
