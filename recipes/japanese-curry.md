# Japanese Curry

tags: Japanese, Curry, Vegetarian, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Potatoes
- Carrots
- Onion
- Fake chicken
- Japanese curry paste or curry blocks
- Rice for serving

## Instructions
1. Peel and dice potatoes, carrots, and onion.
2. If using curry blocks, heat a pan and add oil.
3. Sauté onions until soft, add potatoes and carrots.
4. Add water and simmer until vegetables are tender (10-15 min).
5. Add curry paste/blocks and stir until dissolved.
6. Add tofu or cooked chicken and simmer 5 more minutes.
7. Serve over steamed rice.
