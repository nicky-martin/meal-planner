# Smoothie

tags: Quick, Breakfast, No-Cook, Vegan, Breakfast, Lunch

## Ingredients
- Frozen strawberry
- Oat milk
- Vanilla protein powder
- Banana

## Instructions
1. Add milk to blender first.
2. Add frozen fruit, protein powder, and sweetener.
3. Blend until smooth.
4. Add optional ingredients if desired.
5. Pour and serve immediately.
