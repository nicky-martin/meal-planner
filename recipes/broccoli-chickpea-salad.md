# Broccoli & Chickpea Salad

tags: Salad, Chickpea, Vegetarian, Lunch, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Fresh brocoli
- Canned Greek chickpeas
- Shredded carrots
- Red onion, diced
- Lemon-tahini dressing
- Parsley

## Instructions
1. Chop steamed broccoli and mix with chickpeas.
2. Add carrots and red onion.
3. Dress with lemon-tahini sauce.
4. Season to taste and top with fresh herbs.
5. Serve at room temperature or chilled.
