# Indian Tacos

tags: Indian, Tacos, Fusion, Dinner

## Ingredients
- Flatbread or naan
- Chickpeas or tofu, cooked with Indian spices
- Cilantro
- Red onion
- Mango or apple, diced
- Lime
- Yogurt or cucumber raita
- Jalapeño (optional)

## Instructions
1. Warm the flatbread or naan.
2. Cook chickpeas with curry spices and garlic.
3. Assemble tacos with spiced chickpeas, cilantro, onion, and mango.
4. Add a dollop of yogurt.
5. Serve with lime wedges.
